Reading level难度说明

Words are common nouns and feeling words from RAZ Level C books, plus high-frequency words that are easy to mix up (there / their / where, thought / through). Sentences target Lexile 200L–450L: 9 to 13 words each, joined with because, when, so, but, and after, using past tense and prepositional phrases. They are a step harder than the simple sentences in Level C books.

单词取自 RAZ C 级别书里常见的具体名词和情绪词，另加一组容易混淆的高频词（there / their / where、thought / through 这类）。句子按蓝思 200L–450L 设计，每句 9 到 13 个词，用 because、when、so、but、after 连成复合句，带过去式和介词短语，比 C 级别书里的单句难一档。

English only为什么全英文

There is no translation anywhere. Pictures and sounds take its place, so children link English words directly to what they see and hear. If a picture is unclear, tap the blue “🔊 Listen” button to hear the word.

游戏里不给中文释义，用图和发音代替翻译，让孩子直接把英文和画面、声音对应起来。图看不明白时，点蓝色的「🔊 Listen」就能听到单词读音。

How to play怎么玩

  • Word Challenge: 10 questions per round, three question types mixed together. A wrong answer shows the right one and reads it aloud. Missed words are listed at the end; tap one to hear it again.
    认词挑战：每轮 10 题，看图选词、听音选词、看词选图三种题型混着出。答错会标出正确答案并读一遍，结束后列出错词，点一下可以再听。
  • Listen and Read: each sentence plays once automatically, and each word lights up as it is read. Tap any word to hear it alone. Use “Slow” at the top right to slow down. “Record Me” lets your child record and play back their reading. Tap “I’m Done” after reading.
    听音读句：进题自动播放一遍，读到哪个词哪个词就高亮。点任意单词可以单独听，右上角「Slow」放慢语速。「Record Me」可以录下孩子的朗读再回放对比，读完点「I’m Done」。
  • Picture Fill-in: all three choices fit the grammar, so the picture is the only way to pick the right one. After answering, the whole sentence is read aloud.
    看图填词：三个选项放进句子语法上都通，必须看图才能选对。选完后会把整句读一遍。
  • Each right answer or finished sentence earns a star. Stars are saved on this device.
    每答对一题或读完一句得一颗星，星星累计保存在这台设备上。

Audio发音

All 66 sentences have American English recordings in the audio folder next to this page, so they play offline. Keep the folder with the page if you move it. Single words use online American pronunciation and fall back to the device voice when offline. Recording needs microphone permission in the browser.

66 个句子都预先录好了美式朗读，放在页面旁边的 audio 文件夹里，不联网也能播放，挪动页面时要连文件夹一起带走。单词联网时用美式真人发音，断网时自动换成设备自带的朗读。录音功能需要允许浏览器使用麦克风。
